Privacy Terms

Your money data
stays yours.

Tally is a personal money tracker you run yourself. Log an expense by messaging a Telegram bot — 250 groceries, done — and see where it went in an app that works fully offline. It syncs through your own Google Drive. There is no Tally server, and no account to sign up for.

🤖

Three seconds to log

Message the bot in plain language. It learns your shorthand, so next time it takes zero taps.

📱

Offline first

The app never waits on a network. With no Drive connected at all it is still a complete tracker.

🗄️

Your Drive, your files

Both halves exchange readable JSON in one folder of your Drive. That folder doubles as your backup.

What Tally can see in your Google Drive

Tally asks for exactly one Google permission, drive.file, described by Google as “See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app.” In practice that means:

It can read and write the files it made
One folder named Tally, holding one small JSON snapshot per device — tally-<device-id>.json.
It cannot see anything else you own
Not your documents, not your photos, not files another app created. Google enforces this, not us.
Nothing is sent anywhere else
Your phone and your server talk to Google Drive and to each other through those files. No third party is involved, and neither are we.

There is no “our server”. Tally is software you host. The people who wrote it operate nothing, receive nothing and can see nothing. Read the privacy policy for the detail.

Run it yourself

The whole backend is one Go binary — Telegram bot, Drive sync and SQLite — in one container, and it only ever makes outbound calls. No exposed port, no public hostname, no TLS certificate to renew.
